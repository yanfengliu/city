# Devlog Summary

History before 2026-08-17 lives in `PROGRESS.md` § Log — the phase-by-phase record this devlog supersedes going forward.

- 2026-08-23 (later): **The game learns to wait** — user direction: lower the sensitivity for people to leave, because the game has to be patient while a player builds up gradually. **Measured the complaint before touching a constant:** a starter district (roads + R zoning, nothing else) filled to ~170 people by 23s, lost its first building at 60.3s, and was completely empty by 81s — the game gave a new player one minute to discover plants, lines, pumps, and pipes, then took the whole city. The bad-location grace went 10 -> 60 evaluations (8s -> 48s) and the utility grace 75 -> 300 (60s -> 240s). **The second half of the fix was the shape of the loss, not its timing:** a district loses power on a single tick, so one shared threshold retired every building in the same cadence — a 21-second cliff. `utilityAbandonThreshold` now spreads the grace per building by entity id (integer arithmetic, never `world.random()`, so replay is untouched), and the panel, the roof warning icon, and the rule all read that same per-building value. Re-measured: first loss 60s -> ~250s, empty 81s -> ~350s, and the decline widened from 21s to ~100s of visible drift. Browser-verified: 189 people held for 249s with the warning ramping 0.09 -> 0.99 the whole time, the decline frame shows a MIX of grey and live warning-icon houses rather than a dead block, and a player who reacts keeps all 39 buildings. **`ABANDON_SCORE` 12 -> 8 was tried and reverted:** it did not make the game patient, it deleted the mechanic — the same ruined block then scored 10 and never abandoned at all. Patience belongs in the time to react, not in the threshold. Also landed as a lesson: four "the product is broken" results this session were all a verification scaffold hardcoding coordinates onto procedural terrain. 866 tests.

- 2026-08-23: **Every building gets its own panel, and the inspector learns to fold** — user direction: the resident panel was one crammed column, and buildings had four flat lines. Sections are now individually expandable (stable `id` keys, per-subject collapse state, reused DOM so a 2 Hz live refresh never rebuilds under the pointer), and a new on-demand `inspectBuilding` → `buildingDetail` worker query gives every placed thing a panel tailored to its TYPE: a home in people and who is out of the house, a shop in jobs and shoppers on the way, a school in the children in class and still walking, a plant in capacity against the city's draw. Plants and pumps became selectable at all — their identities now ride the `networks` message. **The rule the design turns on:** `sim/building-detail.ts` restates nothing — `buildingScore`, `buildingEducationOk`, and `utilityTotals` were extracted from `levelSystem` so the panel reads the sim's own arithmetic; a panel that recomputed desirability would drift the first time either side was tuned. **Three defects the rendered result showed that the text did not:** the headline meter, the section header summary, and the section's own meter printed occupancy three times in a row (summaries now render only while collapsed); an already-abandoned building was warned about an abandonment countdown it had already lost; and "Draws 1 units". Browser-verified end to end with real synthetic clicks — 8/8 interaction checks including a click on a coal-plant footprint, the coverage square following a service selection only, bulldozing the inspected building closing its panel, and a collapsed section surviving a live refresh — plus panel screenshots of all 10 subjects. Escape also became a ladder (drag → tool → inspector) so a zone tool falls back to Select. **An independent critic then found three HIGH defects, all the same shape — the panel had become a second opinion on a rule the sim owns:** the Growth section showed a full progress bar on a building `levelSystem` will never advance (missing utilities skip the whole level evaluation, not just its bonus); service coverage counted with exact Chebyshev while the sim marks whole 4-cell blocks, so a garden said "serving 0 buildings" about a home displaying its ✅; and "heading here" counted households that had already arrived or were already walking home, a bug my own test had encoded. All three now read the sim's own rule (`growthBlocker` retraces `levelSystem`'s branch order; `coversCell` is exported from `services.ts`; outings split on phase) and each fix is mutation-checked. The same review proved claim 1 by measurement — 610 score values, `Object.is`, 0 mismatches — and correctly flagged that the rewritten panel had no automated DOM coverage: `jsdom` is now a devDependency and 21 rendered behaviours are pinned, so the focus lesson became a gate and was deleted. 864 tests; **worker budget consciously raised 158k → 166k at 162,585 bytes** for the query.

- 2026-08-19 (later): **The canopy defect becomes a class the gates can see, and a fleet rule** — user direction: record every reported defect and always check it and its relatives, from multiple camera angles and zoom levels, fleet-wide. Landed as two canon rules in `../fleet/FLEET.md` (synced to 23 repos): visual verification sweeps angles and zooms because one framing is not a check, and a reported defect earns an entry in `docs/learning/defect-register.md` plus a check covering its whole CLASS. Here that is `assembly-joints.test.ts` — every primitive of all 10 composed models must form one connected body — and `npm run visual:sweep`, 88 frames over 4 azimuths × 2–3 distances into 11 contact sheets. **The audit found a defect on its first clean run:** the school flag hung off the flagpole's circumradius, which a 6-sided tube only reaches at a vertex. **Three instrument traps, all measured:** the game clamps the camera at 8 units so every previous "close-up" was an 8-unit crop; `CityScene` re-pins `controls.target.y` each frame and drags the camera down by the aim offset, so the frame is not taken from the camera you set (set y=1.22 → photographed from 0.78, ground at 0.86 — underground); and a part-level audit misses a lamp globe floated 0.05, because `part()` bundles primitives under one box. 775 tests; worker unchanged at 157,158 / 158,000.

- 2026-08-19: **Foliage that floated off its trunk** — reported as "tree body is separated from trunk", and it was: `lift` positioned each canopy layer by its NOMINAL height box, but a `DodecahedronGeometry` fills only 93.4% of that box vertically, so the broadleaf's canopy floor sat 0.100 above its trunk top and the columnar's 0.076 — both hovering. `createCanopyGeometry` now seats a layer by its computed lowest vertex, and a new per-archetype `canopySink` settles the whole foliage stack onto the trunk (conifer 0.05 / broadleaf 0.15 / columnar 0.16). **The instrument was the interesting part:** the first version of the contract test measured the WIDEST cross-section at the joint and passed a canopy that still showed daylight — a faceted bottom is an edge, so it reads 0.158 wide from one azimuth and 0.040 from the perpendicular one, and the trees are randomly rotated. Measuring the section's narrowest side instead is what set the shipped sink values. Narrowest section at the trunk top went 0.000 → 0.318 / 0.141 / 0.085 against trunk radii 0.055 / 0.075 / 0.060. Landed as lesson 29. Before/after screenshots at an identical camera; whole-scene sweep clean (park trees are a separate, already-correct code path). 764 tests; worker unchanged at 157,158 / 158,000.

- 2026-08-18 (last): **Routines feed back: D3 lands and the daily-routines plan is complete** — a school arrival credits `completedSchoolTrips` and stamps the HOME BUILDING's `schoolingTick` (stamped on the building, not the household, so the level gate needs no per-tick resident lookup; childless homes stamped by the same morning scan, so the gate reads one uniform field).
